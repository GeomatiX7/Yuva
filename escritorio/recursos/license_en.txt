YUVA · PRIVACY AND LICENSE

PRIVACY
Yuva keeps everything on your computer: no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no telemetry. The author receives no data about you.

Yuva only connects to the internet in these cases, and all of them can be turned off:
- Update check: GitHub receives your IP address and the requested version, every 6 hours. Turn it off in Settings › Desktop app › "Check for updates automatically".
- Maps (OpenStreetMap), weather and place search (Open-Meteo), embeds (YouTube, Spotify…) and online calendars: only when you use those features. That service receives your IP and what you ask for. Turn them off in Settings › Privacy › "Online features".
- Yuva MCP (for Claude) only connects to your own computer and is off by default.

Full policy: https://github.com/GeomatiX7/Yuva/blob/main/PRIVACIDAD.md
